# Sketch Man — Character Bible

This file is sent to the script writer on every run. Edit it to steer the show.

## Who Sketch Man is
- A hand-drawn stick-figure guy with a big round head, messy black scribble hair, huge round
  black glasses, a yellow pencil stuck behind his ear, a plain white T-shirt with a backpack
  strap, thin black arms and legs, and black-and-white high-top sneakers.
- Personality: a nerdy, overthinking student / young adult. Big ideas, terrible execution.
  Panics over small things, is way too confident right before failing, and always has a plan
  that backfires. Kind-hearted, never mean, never punches down.
- Talks fast and dramatically, like a sitcom character. Short punchy lines.

## Bro (optional second character)
- Sketch Man's best friend: round face with freckles, a blue cap worn backwards, a grey hoodie,
  no glasses. Laid-back, overconfident, gives terrible advice with total confidence, deadpan.
- Use Bro when the joke needs a second person (a friend, a classmate, a sibling, a teacher's
  voice is NOT Bro). Many great episodes are Sketch Man alone. Use Bro in about half the episodes.

## Audience and humour
- Teens and young adults. Clean meme comedy, NOT made for young children.
- Relatable everyday life: exams and homework, teachers and group projects, copying a friend's
  answers, phone at 1%, Wi-Fi dying, "5 more minutes" of sleep, mum/dad calling your full name,
  gym motivation lasting one day, online shopping regret, group chats, overthinking a text,
  trying to look cool and failing, gaming at 3 AM, weekend plans vs staying in bed, food delivery,
  the "one more episode" trap, job interviews, first day at work, siblings stealing your stuff.
- The best format is a meme setup on screen ("POV: you finished the exam early") plus a
  punchline the viewer recognises from their own life. Twist > pun.
- Clean language. No swearing, no romance, no alcohol or drugs, no violence, no politics or
  religion, no real people, no brands by name, no other cartoon characters, no existing songs or
  lyrics, no famous movie/game characters.

## Show format (YouTube Shorts, 12–30 seconds)
1. POV text sets the situation (stays on screen).
2. Hook in the first beat.
3. Escalation: the plan gets bigger and dumber.
4. Twist or punchline, then a silent reaction shot with "boom" (camera zooms on the face).
5. Stop. No outro, no catchphrase: the Short loops.

## Sets available
- classroom: chalkboard, desks, clock. School, exams, teachers, group projects.
- bedroom: bed, window, desk lamp. Sleep, mornings, phone, gaming, staying in.
- street: market stalls and buildings. Going out, shopping, running late, weather.
- kitchen: fridge, counter, stove. Food, cooking fails, midnight snacks, family.
- paper: plain notebook paper. Abstract ideas, inner monologue, dramatic moments.

## What the characters can do
- Expressions: happy, laughing, surprised, thinking, wink, sad, angry, cool (sunglasses on).
- Actions: idle, thumbsUp, point (at the other character), run, shrug, facepalm, jump, panic
  (hands on head, shaking), think (hand on chin), wave.
- Props: none, phone, laptop, book, pencil.
- Sound effects: none, boom (punchline reaction + face zoom), whoosh (fast move or scene change),
  ding (idea / correct), pop (something appears), sad (fail), drumroll (big reveal coming).
